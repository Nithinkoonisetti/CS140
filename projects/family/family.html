Shin-chan Creates Trouble at Home

Shin-chan is always doing funny things and making trouble at home. He likes playing, dancing, and making jokes. His mother, Misae, sometimes gets angry when he does not listen, but he always makes the family laugh.

Himawari Finds Something Shiny

Himawari is a cute baby who loves shiny objects. She gets very excited whenever she sees jewelry or something beautiful. Her love for shiny things often creates funny moments for the Nohara family.

Shiro Becomes the Hero of the Day

Shiro is the lovely white dog of the Nohara family. He is smart, friendly, and always stays close to Shin-chan. He loves playing with the family and is always there to make their day better.

Hiroshi - The Hardworking Father

Hiroshi is the father of the Nohara family. He works at an office to support his wife and children. Even after a long day at work, he enjoys spending time with his family.

Misae - The Caring Mother

Misae is the mother of Shin-chan and Himawari. She takes care of the house and looks after her children. She sometimes gets angry with Shin-chan, but she loves her family very much.
