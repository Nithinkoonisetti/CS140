My F1 Hero - Brad Pitt

F1 Movie Brad Pitt

Brad Pitt is a famous American actor who has been in many popular movies. I chose him as my hero because he plays the main character in the F1 movie. I think his role makes the movie more interesting and helps show the excitement of Formula 1 racing.

F1
Brad Pitt plays Sonny Hayes, a former Formula 1 driver.
Fight Club
Brad Pitt plays Tyler Durden, one of the main characters.
World War Z
Brad Pitt plays Gerry Lane, a character who tries to protect his family during a crisis.
