Beaches and Ocean Safety

Hawaiian beaches are beautiful, but conditions can change quickly. Always follow lifeguard instructions and check local alerts. Read Hawai‘i Ocean Safety for current guidance.

Waikīkī, O‘ahu

A well-known coastal district with swimming areas, hotels, and surf lessons. Choose lifeguarded areas when possible.

Image: Unsplash.

North Shore, O‘ahu

Famous for powerful winter surf. Some areas can be extremely hazardous for inexperienced swimmers.

Image: Unsplash.

West and South Maui

Beach conditions vary with weather, wind, and swell. Read posted signs and never turn your back on the ocean.

Image: Unsplash.

Before Entering the Water

Check conditions: Look for lifeguards, warning flags, and beach closures. Avoid swimming alone, and do not enter rough water to take photos.

For weather and marine forecasts, consult the National Weather Service Honolulu forecast office.

Protect Marine Life

Observe animals from a safe distance and never touch coral or marine wildlife. For guidance on viewing marine animals, visit NOAA Fisheries marine-life viewing guidelines.
