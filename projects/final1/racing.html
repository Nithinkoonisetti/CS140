How a Race Weekend Works

A Formula 1 weekend usually includes practice, qualifying, and the main race. Some events also have a sprint format. I like watching qualifying because drivers try to complete a very fast lap.

Practice

During practice sessions, drivers learn about the track and teams test their car setup. They also collect information about tire performance.

Qualifying

Qualifying helps decide the starting order for the race. A faster lap usually gives a driver a better starting position.

The race

Drivers compete over a set distance, and the finishing order decides the race result. Pit stops and safety cars can change the strategy. See the F1 race weekend guide to learn more.
