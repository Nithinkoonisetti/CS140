Famous F1 Circuits

Formula 1 races take place on different types of tracks. Some tracks are built for racing, while others use public streets. Each track has different corners and challenges.

My favorite type of track

I like street circuits because the cars race close to buildings and barriers. Permanent circuits are interesting too because they can have very fast corners.
