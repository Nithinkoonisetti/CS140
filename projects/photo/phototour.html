Welcome to the Vizag City Tour

Welcome to my personal photo tour of Visakhapatnam, popularly known as Vizag. Nestled between the Eastern Ghats mountains and the coast of the Bay of Bengal, Vizag is celebrated as the Jewel of the East Coast. Through this interactive photo tour, you will explore lush mountain vistas, historic naval landmarks, golden coastal beaches, and ancient natural rock formations that make this coastal Indian city unique.

Places on the Tour

  • RK and Rushikonda Beaches — Pristine sea coastline with historic piers and popular surf spots.
  • INS Kursura Submarine Museum — A decommissioned real submarine turned into a beachfront educational museum.
  • Borra Caves — Million-year-old natural limestone caves deep within the Ananthagiri Hills.
  • Fishing Harbour — The energetic center of Vizag's traditional maritime fishing industry.
  • Kailasagiri Hill Park — Panoramic hilltop park offering breathtaking views of the coastline and ocean.
