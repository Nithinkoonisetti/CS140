About the Movie

The Batman is a superhero movie released in 2022. It stars Robert Pattinson as Bruce Wayne.

The story follows Batman as he investigates crimes in Gotham City and tries to stop the Riddler.

Movie Facts

  • Release Year: 2022
  • Director: Matt Reeves
  • Genre: Action and Crime
  • Main Character: Batman
  • Setting: Gotham City
