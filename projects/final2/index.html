Welcome to Formula 1

Welcome to my website about Formula 1! I chose this topic because I think racing cars and the competition between drivers are interesting. On this website, I will explain teams, circuits, technology, and how a race weekend works.

What is Formula 1?

Formula 1 is a racing championship where drivers compete in specially designed cars. The races take place at different tracks around the world. Teams work with drivers, engineers, and mechanics to try to get the best result. You can read more on the official F1 beginner guide.

What you can find here

Use the menu to learn about some well-known teams, famous tracks, race car technology, and the rules of a race weekend. You can also fill out a short fan form.
