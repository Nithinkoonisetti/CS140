Teams and Drivers

In Formula 1, drivers race for teams. A team normally has two race drivers, and many other people help prepare the cars. I think the teamwork is one of the most interesting parts of the sport.

Some famous teams

Ferrari is one of the best-known names in Formula 1. The team is famous for its red cars and long history. Read more on the official Ferrari team page.

McLaren is another historic team. It is well known for its orange racing cars. Learn about it on the official McLaren team page.

Mercedes has also had many successful years in the sport. Visit the official Mercedes team page for more details.

What does a driver do?

A driver needs concentration, quick reactions, and good communication with the team. During a race, the driver also needs to manage tires and follow the team's strategy.
